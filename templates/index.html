What is Gen-Z English Slang?

Gen-Z English Slang refers to the informal language and expressions used by the Generation Z demographic, typically born between the mid-1990s and early 2010s. This slang often includes abbreviations, acronyms, and unique phrases that have emerged from social media platforms.

Examples of Gen-Z English Slang:

  • Lit: Used to describe something exciting or excellent.
  • Salty: Feeling bitter or upset about something.
  • GOAT: Acronym for "Greatest of All Time."
  • Flex: To show off or boast about something.
  • Tea: Gossip or interesting news.
